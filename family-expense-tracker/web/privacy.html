Privacy Policy

Hearth — private family finance app. Last updated: 26 July 2026.

What this app is

Hearth is a private, self-hosted household expense tracker used by one family. It is not a commercial service and has no other users.

What data it stores

Receipts, bills, and bank transactions belonging to the household, stored in the household's own Supabase (PostgreSQL) project in the EU. Documents are analyzed by Anthropic's Claude API solely to extract amounts and categories.

Bank data

Bank account information is accessed read-only via Enable Banking's PSD2-compliant API, only after the account holder explicitly authorizes it at their bank, and only for the household's own accounts. Consents expire per PSD2 rules and can be revoked at the bank at any time.

Sharing

Data is never sold or shared with third parties beyond the processors named above.
