# FachCoach v2: build prompt for Claude Code on the Mac Studio

You are building FachCoach on this Mac Studio: an exam coach for Cashmy, a gynaecologist preparing for the oral Facharztprüfung Frauenheilkunde und Geburtshilfe at the Bezirksärztekammer Nordwürttemberg. Her surface is **GynDesk**, a web app she adds to the Home Screen of her iPad from Safari and uses like a native app. There is no chat bot and no Google Doc in this design. Read this whole file before you touch anything. RadCoach (`~/RadCoach`, spec in `~/RadCoach-spec/PROMPT.md`) already exists on this Mac; reuse its library code where this file says so, and keep FachCoach's state, secrets and daemons completely separate.

## 0. How to work on this
- **Who it is for:** Cashmy Joy, 36, completes her 60 months of Weiterbildung on 31.10.2026; the oral exam will be scheduled by the Kammer after the logbook is signed, expected late January to March 2027. She works in a Praxis Mon 11:00–19:30, Tue 08:00–12:00, Wed 08:00–15:30, Fri 08:00–17:30; Thursday is free; three children at home; Urlaub 21.12.2026–10.01.2027 is her intensive block. Realistic study time: about 12 hours a week, mostly evenings after 20:00, Thursdays and weekends, often away from home (Praxis, commute, in-laws). She reads textbooks as PDFs in Notability on the iPad and highlights with the Apple Pencil, and she reads AMBOSS.
- **Who commissions it:** Ajit, her husband, who runs this Mac. Talk to him in English. Everything she sees in GynDesk is in **German** (the exam language).
- **You do the work.** Build and test everything end to end yourself. Collect everything that needs a human (one-click approvals, consent, the ten minutes with her iPad, anything paid) into one list per phase. Ask Ajit before anything paid. Report per phase in three sections: **VERIFIED ON DEVICE / STATICALLY CHECKED / BLOCKED**.
- **Dates** as calendar dates, never calendar-week numbers.
- **Ajit's own study app (RadDesk)** was planned for this Mac. Ask Ajit for its folder; if it exists, reuse its layout, components and conventions wherever they fit, so both apps feel like one family. If it does not exist yet, build GynDesk with the stack in section 6 and keep it reusable for RadDesk later.

## 1. Non-negotiable rules
1. **No patient identifiers anywhere.** She will mention cases from her Praxis and her Logbuch. The privacy gate (reuse `~/RadCoach/radcoach/privacy.py` or its equivalent) runs on every text, transcript and image before storage or any model call: name, date of birth, patient ID, exact dates of care → refuse and ask her to rephrase. Age only as a decade.
2. **Her own words are the gold.** Questions, cards and summaries are built from her highlights, her handwritten margin notes and her own takeaways. The model may explain and grade; it never invents a "fact" into her material without a guideline citation. In articles, her sentences stay verbatim and are marked as hers; model additions are marked and cited.
3. **Guidelines are the ground truth.** Grading of her answers cites the guideline and section (AWMF/DGGG S2k/S3 Leitlinien, G-BA Mutterschafts-Richtlinien, Krebsfrüherkennungs-Richtlinie, STIKO where relevant). A grade without a citation is not a grade.
4. **AMBOSS is never automated.** No scraping, no login automation. She reads AMBOSS herself and puts takeaways into the Merke box.
5. **Textbooks stay private.** Highlight extraction is for her personal study only; store extracted snippets, never whole pages or chapters; never upload the PDFs anywhere; GynDesk shows a snippet with book and page, never the page image.
6. **Nothing runs in the Hermes Docker sandbox, nothing scans iCloud or Google Drive for desktop file-provider paths from a daemon loop.** Host launchd daemons, like RadCoach. Notability backups are fetched through the Drive API (or WebDAV, section 3) onto local disk.
7. **Separate from RadCoach:** own folder `~/FachCoach`, own SQLite, own secrets (mode 600), own launchd labels `com.ajit.fachcoach-*`, own port. Import RadCoach modules as a library; never modify RadCoach files.
8. **Model budget:** this runs on Ajit's Claude Max 20x subscription alongside RadCoach. FachCoach uses `claude-opus-5-5` for grading, question generation, articles and summaries, `claude-haiku-5-5` for classification, routing and term explanations, and never Fable. Use `claude -p` with `--output-format json --json-schema`, `--permission-prompts none`, no `--bare` (reuse RadCoach's runner, `~/RadCoach/radcoach/claude_runner.py` or equivalent, including its cost logging and rate-limit fallback). Keep FachCoach's share small: batch work in the nightly run, grade on demand, cache every term explanation.
9. **GynDesk is on the public internet (section 2), so it is hardened like a public service:** nothing but the login page, the manifest and the icons is reachable without a session; passkey login; rate limits; secure cookies; CSRF on every POST; no content in logs; a failed login never reveals whether the user exists.
10. **Her data is hers.** Ajit's admin page shows system health only. Her progress is visible to him only if she switches on „Fortschritt mit Ajit teilen“ in Einstellungen (default off).
11. **No dependency on the gaming PC.** She studies evenings and weekends when the PC is asleep. Everything FachCoach needs runs on this Mac.

## 2. Her surface: GynDesk, a Home-Screen web app
### 2.1 What it is and why
- A progressive web app served from this Mac. She opens the URL once in Safari on the iPad, taps Teilen → „Zum Home-Bildschirm“, and from then on it is an app icon with its own window, push notifications, an app badge, microphone access for mock exams and an offline copy of today's drill. The same works on her iPhone.
- Why not Telegram or WhatsApp: she does not want to study inside a chat. Why not a native app: no App Store, no TestFlight, no Xcode, and every change ships the moment the Mac restarts the service.
- iPad first: landscape shows three columns (outline | content | term panel), portrait stacks them with a bottom tab bar. Large touch targets, dark mode, adjustable font size. System font; no web fonts.

### 2.2 Reach: it must work from anywhere, never LAN-only
- Serve on `127.0.0.1:8791` and publish it with **Tailscale Funnel**: `tailscale funnel --bg 8791` gives `https://<mac-hostname>.<tailnet>.ts.net` with a valid certificate, no domain, no new account, free on the personal plan. Funnel needs MagicDNS, HTTPS certificates and the `funnel` node attribute enabled in the Tailscale admin console; the command prints the approval link when something is missing. Those clicks are Ajit's (needs-human). The Mac App Store build keeps the CLI at `/Applications/Tailscale.app/Contents/MacOS/Tailscale`; the standalone build has `tailscale` on PATH.
- Alternative only if Ajit prefers a nicer URL and owns a domain: Cloudflare Tunnel (free). Ask him; do not set up a domain or any paid service yourself.
- Funnel terminates TLS on this Mac and forwards plain HTTP to the app: set `Secure`, `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax` cookies anyway, trust only the local proxy for `X-Forwarded-*`, and redirect any non-HTTPS origin.
- Make the service survive reboots: `tailscale funnel --bg` is persistent; the app runs under launchd with KeepAlive.

### 2.3 Login
- **Passkey (Face ID)**: WebAuthn with the `py_webauthn` library, `rp.id` = the public hostname. Enrolment through a single-use link (also as a QR code printed to the terminal) that Ajit opens on her iPad once; she registers the passkey with Face ID. Sessions last 180 days, renewed on use.
- **Fallback**: a long passphrase (8 random German words, shown to Ajit once, stored hashed) for a new device or a broken passkey. Rate limit: 5 attempts per hour per IP.
- Two users: `cashmy` (owner) and `ajit` (admin: health, queue, costs, ingestion log; her content only with her sharing switch on).

### 2.4 iOS facts the build must respect (verify each on her iPad in the Phase 1 device test)
- Web Push and the app badge work only after the app is added to the Home Screen and she taps „Benachrichtigungen erlauben“ once inside the app. Ask for the permission on a tap, never on page load. Server side: `pywebpush` with VAPID keys in `~/FachCoach/secrets/`.
- No background work on the iPad: no Background Sync, no periodic sync. Everything scheduled runs on this Mac; the app only shows results and queues her answers in IndexedDB while offline, flushing them on the next open.
- Microphone: `getUserMedia` + `MediaRecorder` produce `audio/mp4` on iOS. Record in 30-second chunks, upload as they finish, cap a single answer at 3 minutes. Keep the screen awake during an exam with the Screen Wake Lock API.
- Spoken questions use the device's own `speechSynthesis` with a `de-DE` voice (offline, free, no latency); the first utterance must follow a tap. Provide a mute switch and a replay button.
- No Web Share Target on iOS. For AMBOSS takeaways she pastes into the Merke box; optionally a 3-action iOS Shortcut „An GynDesk senden“ (Receive input from Share Sheet → Get Contents of URL (POST with a bearer token) → Show Result) for text she selects anywhere. No logic in the Shortcut; the server does everything.
- Home-Screen web apps are exempt from Safari's 7-day storage eviction, but the server remains the only source of truth; the client caches, it never owns data.
- Manifest: `display: "standalone"`, `start_url: "/heute"`, `scope: "/"`, icons 180/192/512 (also `apple-touch-icon`), `theme_color`, `viewport-fit=cover` with safe-area insets. Service worker: app shell cached, content stale-while-revalidate, today's drill and the current article pre-cached.

### 2.5 Pages (German UI; routes in English are fine)
- **Heute** (`/heute`): the ten due questions of the day, one at a time. Answer modes: think and reveal → self-grade „Nochmal / Verstanden / Leicht“ (default, free); type or dictate an answer and tap „Bewerten lassen“ (Opus grades with citations). Below: the two topics of the week, today's highlight count, the next mock exam.
- **Prüfung** (`/pruefung`): the mock Fachgespräch. Pick a topic or „Gemischt“; the examiner question appears and is spoken; big record button; her transcript is shown and editable before grading (her words are gold; dictation mangles terms); grading with score, missed points with guideline citations, then the examiner's follow-up, up to three rounds; scorecard at the end with the cited passages. Scheduled slots Tue 15:00, Thu 10:00, Sat 10:00 arrive as push reminders; she can start one any time.
- **Markierungen** (`/markierungen`): every highlight as a card: snippet, book, page, colour, her margin note, status (neu / gelernt / sicher), when it was last asked. Filters by book, topic, colour, status. One tap: „Frage dazu“, „Als Merke speichern“, „Falsch erkannt“ (marks the extraction as wrong and hides it).
- **Themen** (`/themen`): the topic map and, per topic, an **article page laid out like AMBOSS**: left outline, collapsible sections in a fixed order (Definition und Epidemiologie, Ätiologie und Risikofaktoren, Klinik, Diagnostik, Therapie, Komplikationen, Aufklärung und Recht, Leitlinie und Quellen, Prüfungsfragen), built from her highlights and notes plus guideline passages; each section ends with its Q&A; term tooltips in the text; a „Bild per Link“ and „Video“ button per section; a mastery bar per topic.
- **Begriffe** (the right panel on every content page): tap any term → a short German explanation (Haiku, cached forever); explained terms stay as clickable chips, per topic and in a global list, so she builds her own glossary without typing.
- **Merke** (`/merke`, the „+“ box): paste or dictate anything (an AMBOSS takeaway, a Praxis insight without identifiers, a summary of a chapter). The server: privacy gate → Opus summarises in her structure, cross-checks against the guideline index and flags discrepancies, suggests the topic, generates Q&A → she confirms or edits before it becomes part of her material.
- **Leitlinien** (`/leitlinien`): the corpus with version and date, searchable; every citation in the app links here.
- **Fortschritt** (`/fortschritt`): mastery per topic, error streaks, time per answer, the weekly report (Sunday 18:00), the exam countdown and the plan (section 5).
- **Einstellungen**: notification times, exam date (`Prüfungstermin`), voice on/off, font size, „Fortschritt mit Ajit teilen“, passkey management, Shortcut token.
- **/admin** (Ajit): health, last ingestion, queue, model costs, Funnel status, logs without content.

### 2.6 Parity with RadDesk
These are the features of Ajit's own app that must exist here too: paste content → AI summary, cross-check, suggested topic and Q&A; the AMBOSS-style article layout with left outline, collapsible sections in fixed order and term tooltips; the term panel with explained terms kept as clickable chips; every topic organised as Q&A; the „+“ box to add a topic with her own summary; per-section picture-by-link and video buttons. If RadDesk exists on this Mac, read its code for anything else it does and port what makes sense for an exam candidate.

## 3. Architecture
### Tier 0: Mac, Python, launchd, no model
- **Notability intake.** Option A (default): she enables Notability → Auto-backup → Google Drive, PDF, in her own Google account and shares the backup folder with Ajit's account; the **Drive watcher** (every 10 min) lists that folder through the Drive API (`drive.readonly` scope on FachCoach's own OAuth token, same Google client JSON as RadCoach, separate consent), downloads new or changed PDFs to `~/FachCoach/inbox/` (local disk), records file id, md5 and modified time in SQLite. Option B, only if Notability's Auto-backup settings on her iPad offer WebDAV (Ajit checks in ten seconds): run a small WebDAV endpoint under the Funnel URL at `/dav/` with its own basic-auth credentials, writing into `~/FachCoach/inbox/`; then no Google consent is needed at all. Build A; add B if the check says yes.
- **Highlight extractor**: for each changed PDF, render pages at 150 dpi (PyMuPDF), detect highlighter colour masks (Notability's translucent yellow, pink, green, blue, orange; HSV thresholds, morphological closing, connected components → bounding boxes), extract the text under each box from the PDF text layer (`page.get_text("words")`, clipped), and OCR the box with Apple Vision (pyobjc) when there is no text layer. Handwritten margin notes (Pencil ink that is not highlighter colour): OCR with Apple Vision handwriting recognition, stored as her notes, with a confidence; low-confidence notes are shown to her for correction, never silently used. Only changed pages are reprocessed (page image hash). Output: knowledge units (book, chapter if detectable, page, highlight text, her note, colour, timestamp) in SQLite.
- **Queue, scheduler, health, alerts**: as in RadCoach (reuse). Health alert to Ajit's alertd Telegram if the watcher, the web app or the Funnel endpoint is down for 15 min (check the public URL from outside the tailnet with a plain HTTPS request to `/health`, which returns a version string and nothing else).
- **Record**: `~/FachCoach/{inbox, units, questions, sessions, audio, ledger, reports}`; everything de-identified; audio deleted after transcription unless she keeps it.

### Tier 1: local, deterministic first
- Privacy gate on all incoming text, transcripts and images.
- **Guideline corpus**: download the current AWMF guideline PDFs for Gynäkologie und Geburtshilfe (register 015-xxx), the Mutterschafts-Richtlinien and the Krebsfrüherkennungs-Richtlinie into `~/FachCoach/guidelines/` (public documents; record version and date), split into sections, build a local embedding index with the Studio's Ollama (an embedding model such as `nomic-embed-text` or `bge-m3`; pull it if missing). Refresh monthly; a guideline updated in the last 24 months gets an „aktualisiert“ badge, because examiners love asking about changes.
- **Topic map** (the Prüfungskatalog, stored as data so Ajit or she can edit it; each topic has an exam weight): Geburtshilfe-Notfälle (Schulterdystokie, postpartale Blutung, Präeklampsie/Eklampsie/HELLP, vorzeitiger Blasensprung, Frühgeburt und Lungenreife, CTG-Beurteilung, Beckenendlage, Sectio-Indikationen, Uterusruptur), Schwangerenvorsorge und Pränataldiagnostik (Mutterschafts-Richtlinien, Ersttrimester-Screening, Gestationsdiabetes, Rhesus, Infektionen in der Schwangerschaft), Gynäkologische Onkologie (Mamma, Zervix, Endometrium, Ovar, Vulva: Früherkennung, Staging, leitliniengerechte Therapie, Nachsorge), Endokrinologie und Reproduktion (Zyklusstörungen, PCOS, Amenorrhoe, Kinderwunsch, Kontrazeption, Klimakterium/HRT), Urogynäkologie (Inkontinenz, Deszensus), Infektionen und Sexualmedizin (STI, HPV, Vaginose, Impfungen), Operative Gynäkologie und Komplikationen, Mamma-Diagnostik, Notfälle (Extrauteringravidität, Ovarialtorsion, akutes Abdomen), Recht und Ethik (§218 StGB, Mutterschutz, Aufklärung), Kinder- und Jugendgynäkologie. Each knowledge unit and each question is classified into one topic (Haiku, or the local model).
- **Voice**: transcription on this Mac with `mlx-whisper` (`mlx-community/whisper-large-v3-turbo`, German, Apple Silicon) or whisper.cpp if mlx is unavailable; measure the time for a 60-second answer and report it. Spoken questions come from the iPad's own `speechSynthesis`, so no TTS runs on the Mac.
- **Nightly optimizer** (03:00, deterministic, no model): recompute a priority score per unit and per topic from exam weight × her error rate × days since last seen × highlight density (what she highlights a lot is what she thinks matters) × guideline recency; rebuild **Heute** (ten questions: six due repetitions, three from the week's topics, one from a weak topic she has not seen for a while) and **Hochrelevant** (the top twenty units across the map, shown on Fortschritt); retire mastered units to the monthly cycle; queue question generation for units that have none. Spacing: wrong answers return after 1, 3, 7, 14 days; correct ones stretch 1 → 3 → 7 → 14 → 30 → 60 days. A topic is mastered after 5 consecutive correct answers in drills and one mock exam above 80 %.

### Tier 2: Claude (Opus and Haiku), batched and on demand
- **Question generation**: when a backup has been ingested (batched, at most once per hour) and in the nightly run: from new unit clusters → 3–8 questions per cluster in exam style („Eine 32-jährige Zweitgebärende in der 38. SSW … Wie gehen Sie vor?“), each with model answer, guideline citation, difficulty and topic. Stored; the optimizer decides when they appear.
- **Grading** (on demand): drill answers when she taps „Bewerten lassen“; every mock-exam answer: content, structure (Anamnese → Diagnostik → Therapie → Aufklärung), completeness, red flags named; missed points each with a citation; then the examiner's follow-up question. JSON schema enforced.
- **Articles** (Themen): built once per topic from her units plus guideline passages, rebuilt when her material for the topic grows by more than ten units or she asks; her sentences verbatim and marked.
- **Merke processing** and **term explanations** as in 2.5.
- **Weekly report** (Sunday 18:00, push + Fortschritt): score per topic, repeated gaps, time per answer, mastery, next week's two topics.

## 6. Engineering
- Python 3.12 in `~/FachCoach/.venv`: FastAPI + uvicorn, Jinja2 templates, `py_webauthn`, `pywebpush`, PyMuPDF, Pillow, numpy, pyobjc (Vision), google-api-python-client, `mlx-whisper`; RadCoach imported by path. SQLite with WAL; one process for the web app, separate launchd daemons for watcher and scheduler.
- Front end: server-rendered pages plus small vanilla ES modules and one service worker; no bundler, no CDN, no web fonts; everything must work offline from the cache. Keep the JS under 60 KB total.
- Ports: app `127.0.0.1:8791` (RadCoach's planned PC worker uses 8790; do not collide). Funnel on 443.
- `fachcoachctl`: `status`, `ingest-now`, `optimize-now`, `exam <topic>`, `report`, `pause`, `resume`, `termin <date>`, `enrol <user>` (prints the single-use link and QR), `funnel-check` (fetches `/health` through the public URL).
- launchd: `com.ajit.fachcoach-web`, `-drive-watcher`, `-scheduler`; KeepAlive; hung-poll self-kill as in RadCoach.
- Tests: highlight detection on a synthetic PDF with coloured boxes over known text; OCR fallback; privacy gate; question schema; optimizer scoring and spacing; mock-exam state machine; WebAuthn registration and login with Playwright's virtual authenticator in Chromium; passphrase login, manifest, service-worker registration and offline Heute in Playwright WebKit; push send against a mock endpoint; `/health` through the Funnel URL from outside the tailnet (use a plain `curl` without the tailnet, or report BLOCKED if you cannot leave it). The real iPad test is Ajit's ten minutes with her (needs-human): add to Home Screen, Face ID, allow notifications, one spoken answer.
- No secrets, no extracted textbook text and no audio in git; `~/FachCoach` is its own repo with a `.gitignore` for `inbox/ units/ audio/ secrets/ state/`.

## 8. Definition of done, Phase 1
1. A new Notability backup appears → within 15 min its highlights are in Markierungen with book, page and the correct text.
2. A page with highlights over a scanned (no text layer) page yields correct text via OCR.
3. A Merke entry containing a fake patient name is refused and nothing is stored.
4. Heute shows ten questions built from her highlights, each with a guideline citation; a self-grade changes the next due date as specified; the push at 20:30 arrives on a Home-Screen install (verified on Ajit's iPhone before her iPad).
5. The public URL serves the login page with a valid certificate from outside the tailnet; every other route returns 401 without a session; passkey login works in the Chromium test and on the device test.
6. The service worker serves Heute while the device is offline, and an answer given offline is stored after reconnecting.
7. Killing the web app triggers the alert within 15 min.
8. A one-page needs-human list: Funnel approval clicks (MagicDNS, HTTPS, funnel attribute), Notability backup enabled (and the WebDAV check), Drive consent for FachCoach's own token if Option A, her ten minutes on the iPad, her consent to Claude processing her study material, the exam date when known.
