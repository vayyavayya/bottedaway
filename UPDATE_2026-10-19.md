## 3. Correction format (rendered order)

1. Bold heading line when the case has no „Correction:“ label: **Correction (Case N: <exam>, <question>):**

3. **Corrected Beurteilung:** first, with highlights. Line 1 answers the question of the request.
4. **Corrected Befund:** his sentences with highlights (struck red / yellow / green, colours as in `PROMPT.md` section 4).

6. **Merke:** ≤3 lines.
7. **Unsure:** what cannot be judged on screenshots, and „if the signed report differs, paste it“.
8. **Privacy:** only if needed (section 6).

- **Revision guard while he types.** `requiredRevisionId` failed three times in a row while he was typing. Ladder:
  1. Use `requiredRevisionId` with re-read and re-anchoring by paragraph text, up to 3 tries.
  2. Then use `targetRevisionId` from the latest read; Google transforms the indexes over his concurrent edits.
  3. Allowed only for inserts whose anchor paragraph is not the one he is editing, and always followed by a verifying re-read. Never send a batch without one of the two guards.
